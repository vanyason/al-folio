Selected long-form interviews and conversations

Long-form public conversations on U.S. history, Russian-American relations, political memory, and contemporary Russia.

  • 2026 — Russian Neutrality in the American Revolution: Fear, Strategy, and Opportunity, video podcast interview with Adel Aali for Analyzing American Revolution, on Imperial Russia, the American Revolution, Catherine the Great, armed neutrality, and the early history of Russian-American relations. In English. Listen / Read.
  • 2024 — Shameful Questions About America / "Стыдные вопросы про Америку / вДудь", long-form interview with Yuri Dud / вДудь. A conversation on U.S. history, American identity, and Russian-American relations. In Russian, with English subtitles. Watch on YouTube (5.0 million views).
  • 2022 — When Did the United States and Russia Fall Out, Who Started It, and Will There Be War with America? / "Иван Курилла: когда поссорились США и Россия, кто первым начал и будет ли война с Америкой?", long-form interview with Irina Shikhman / А поговорить?. A conversation on the history of U.S.-Russian relations, American politics, and the changing images of America and Russia. In Russian. Watch on YouTube (1.4 million views).
  • 2024 — Trump's Victory: What Will Happen to Russia, Ukraine, and China? / "Победа Трампа. Что будет с Россией, Украиной и Китаем? / Курилла, Иноземцев", long-form interview with Voice of America Russian, hosted by Marianna Minsker. A conversation on the uses of history, Russian public discourse, and U.S.-Russian relations after the 2024 U.S. presidential election. In Russian. Watch on YouTube (200,000 views).
