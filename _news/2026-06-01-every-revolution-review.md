My [review](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/03612759.2026.2676416) of Aren Lerner Craig's _Every Revolution Was First a Thought: The Civil War and Transcendentalism in Transatlantic Context_ was published in _History: Reviews of New Books_. The book explores the intellectual and philosophical languages through which contemporaries understood the American Civil War.
